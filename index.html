Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

You'll need a minimum of 18 feet wide, 15 feet deep, and 10.5 feet of ceiling clearance. A typical two-car garage, large living room, basement, or event room works great. If you're unsure, send us your dimensions and we'll let you know.
Every package includes full delivery and professional setup, the impact screen and projector, a launch monitor with shot data, a commercial hitting mat, men's, women's, and junior club sets, GSPro software with 100+ courses, on-call support, and teardown and pickup when you're finished.
Not at all. Most customers are there for the experience, not to shoot par. GSPro has a practice range mode, and total beginners have a blast. If you've never held a club, we'll point you in the right direction at setup.
GSPro supports groups of up to 8 players per round, with others watching on screen or TV. For larger groups, players rotate in. Most events with 10–15 people flow great over a 3–4 hour rental.
We primarily serve the Louisville metro area including Louisville, Jeffersontown, St. Matthews, Shively, and surrounding communities. Contact us if you're outside our primary range — for the right event, we'll make it work.
Weekend slots — especially Friday and Saturday evenings — book fast. We recommend at least 2 weeks in advance for weekends and 1 week for weekdays. For holidays and major events, book as early as possible.
Full refund for cancellations 72+ hours before your booking. Cancellations within 48–72 hours receive a credit toward a future booking. Cancellations within 24 hours are non-refundable. Weather and emergencies are handled case by case.
We don't staff an on-site attendant for standard rentals — we set up, get you running, and stay reachable by phone the entire time. If you'd like an on-site attendant for your event, ask about our premium add-on when you book.
