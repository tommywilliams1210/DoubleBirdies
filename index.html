We Deliver & Set Up

Not at all. Most customers are there for the experience, not the scorecard. GSPro has a practice range mode and total beginners have a blast. If you've never picked up a club, we'll point you in the right direction at setup.
GSPro supports up to 8 players per round, with others watching on screen or TV display. For larger groups, players rotate in. Most events with 10-15 people flow great over a 3-4 hour rental.
We serve the Louisville metro area and Lexington, KY. If you're between the two or just outside, reach out - for the right event we'll make it work.
Friday and Saturday evenings book fast - we recommend at least 2 weeks out for weekends and 1 week for weekdays. Holidays and major events book even earlier.
Full refund for cancellations 72+ hours before your booking. Within 48-72 hours, you receive a credit toward a future booking. Within 24 hours, non-refundable. Weather and genuine emergencies handled case by case.
Standard rentals don't include an on-site attendant - we set up, get you running, and stay reachable by phone. If you'd like someone on-site, ask about our attendant add-on when you book.
Yes. Our club sets are there for anyone who needs them, but if you want to use your own sticks, go for it. The launch monitor works with any clubs.
