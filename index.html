How It Works

Everything Handled. You Just Play.

Double Birdie drops off a fully self-contained golf simulator at your location. No venue. No staff on-site. Just drop-off, setup, and a full round on GSPro.

01

Book Online

Pick your package, date, and time. We confirm within 24 hours, collect your event address, and walk you through space requirements.

02

We Deliver & Set Up

Our team arrives 45–60 minutes before your start time. We build out the full rig — screen, projector, launch monitor, clubs, hitting mat, and GSPro software — and get you playing before we leave.

03

You Play

Choose from 100+ courses on GSPro. Competitive stroke play, casual rounds, or practice range — your call. We stay reachable by phone throughout. When your time is up, we come back and break everything down.

GSPro supports up to 8 players per round, with others watching on screen or the TV display. For larger groups players rotate in. Most events with 10–15 people flow great over a 3–4 hour rental.
We serve the Louisville metro area and Lexington, KY. If you're between the two or just outside, reach out — for the right event we'll make it work.
Friday and Saturday evenings book fast — we recommend at least 2 weeks out for weekends, 1 week for weekdays. Holidays and major events book even earlier. The sooner the better.
Full refund for cancellations 72+ hours before your booking. Cancellations within 48–72 hours receive a credit toward a future booking. Cancellations within 24 hours are non-refundable. Weather and genuine emergencies are handled case by case — just reach out.
Standard rentals don't include an on-site attendant — we set up, get you running, and stay reachable by phone the entire time. If you'd like someone on-site for your event, ask about our attendant add-on when you book.
